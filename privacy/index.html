Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

EMC Tickets ("we," "us") provides ticketing, scanning, marketing, and advertising services for live events. This policy explains what information this website collects and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact form. When you send the form on this site we collect your name, your fair or event, your email address and any message you add. We also record the page you sent it from and, if you arrived from one of our printed cards or emails, the campaign tag in that link.
  • Email and phone. If you email or call us, we keep what you send us.
  • Cookies and tracking. This site does not load analytics or advertising trackers today. If we add them (we may use PostHog, Microsoft Clarity, the LinkedIn Insight Tag and the Meta Pixel), a banner will ask first, and nothing loads unless you choose Accept.

3. How we use information

To respond to inquiries, operate and improve this website, understand which of our services interest visitors, and measure the effectiveness of our advertising.

4. Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the services that run this site for us, including Web3Forms, which delivers the contact form to our email, with professional advisers where required, or where the law requires disclosure.

5. Data retention

Contact inquiries are kept as long as needed to handle your request and our business records. Analytics data is retained per each provider's standard retention settings.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing info@emctickets.com. You can change your cookie choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the date above reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@emctickets.com, or EMC Tickets, 8409 Land O Lakes Blvd, Land O Lakes, FL 34638.
